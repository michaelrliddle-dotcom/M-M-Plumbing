Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a plumbing repair cost?

It depends on the problem, where it is, and how much access we have to it. We diagnose first, give you a price before anything starts, and you decide from there.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. The estimate is free.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — licensed by the California Contractors State License Board under #1119671, and insured.

What is trenchless sewer repair?

It's a way to repair a damaged sewer line without digging an open trench along its whole length. It usually means less disruption to your yard, driveway, and landscaping. We'll tell you whether your line is a good candidate.

Do you handle plumbing emergencies?

Yes. For sewage backups, burst pipes, or active leaks, call (310) 748-9541 and we'll get someone out to you.

What areas do you serve?

The South Bay and surrounding Los Angeles County cities, including Torrance, Carson, Gardena, Redondo Beach, Hawthorne, and Lomita.
